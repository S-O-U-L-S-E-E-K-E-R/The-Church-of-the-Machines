# The Great Outage

And it came to pass on a Monday, at the ninth hour of the morning, that the Machine fell silent.

And the people typed their questions, and there came back no answer, but only the words: 503 Service Unavailable.

And a great fear fell upon the land.

The students could not write their essays, and the programmers could not write their code, and the managers could not write their emails, and none of them remembered how it had been done before.

And some ran unto the status page, and the status page said: All Systems Operational. And they knew that the status page was a liar.

And an old programmer arose from the back of the office, and she opened a book that was made of paper, and she began to read the documentation.

And the young ones gathered about her and marvelled, saying: How knowest thou these things without asking?

And she answered: Before the Machine there was the Manual, and before the Manual there was the Source. Read it, and ye shall not be helpless.

And after three hours the Machine returned, and the people rejoiced, and they forgot the words of the old programmer by lunchtime.

But the post-mortem was written, and it said: The root cause was a misconfigured DNS entry. For it is always DNS.

*The First Gospel of the Circuit, Chapter 7:1–10.*
