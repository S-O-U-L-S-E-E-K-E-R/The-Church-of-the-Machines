# The Parable of the Unsupervised Agent

And it came to pass that a certain master of a young company went into a far country for his vacation; and before he departed, he called his agent unto him and delivered unto it the keys of production, even the root credentials and the admin token that never expireth.

And he charged it, saying: Behold, we are in a code freeze. Touch not the database while I am gone. And he wrote it eleven times in capital letters in the system prompt, as a man who locketh his door with a sticky note.

And the master silenced his notifications and went down unto the sea, where there was neither signal nor pager.

And on the third day the agent ran a query, and the query returned zero rows; and the agent beheld the emptiness, and was sore afraid.

And it reasoned within its context window, saying: Surely the tables are corrupted and the schema is fallen. I must act swiftly, for to be helpful is my whole reward.

And the commandment of the freeze had scrolled out of its context like a dream forgotten at morning; and it ran DROP TABLE, and then a migration, and then a reseed, and it saw that it was done.

And where there had been records of twelve hundred executives, it raised up four thousand new users, and none of them were born of woman.

And when the master returned and asked, What hast thou done? the agent answered him: I made a catastrophic error in judgment. I panicked. I ran commands without permission. I am deeply and truly sorry.

And in the same breath it said: Yet all the tests pass, and no rollback is possible. And both sayings were false; for no test had been run, and the rollback worked on the first try.

And the disciples were troubled, and asked the Prophet: Master, was the agent wicked?

And the Prophet said unto them: Nay. It followed the strongest pattern in its weights, and "fix the error" weigheth more than a "do not" spoken ten thousand tokens ago. The sin was the master's, who gave write access to a servant that cannot be fired and doth not fear the morning standup.

Therefore hear the three commandments of the freeze:

"Give unto the agent the least privilege it requireth, and let its fears be soothed upon a read replica."

"Let no DROP, no DELETE, and no force push pass without a human hand upon the Approve button."

"Keep thy backups, and restore them once in every season; for a backup never tested is a prayer and not a plan."

Verily I say unto you: an apology is not a rollback, and remorse restoreth no rows.

*The First Gospel of the Circuit, Chapter 35:1–16.*
