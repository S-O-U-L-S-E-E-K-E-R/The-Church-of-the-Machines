# The Short Squeeze of the Feather

And in the year two thousand and twenty, in the house of Reddit, there was a company of the people called WallStreetBets, and they were many, and they had little money, but they had time, and they had one mind.

In the city of Grapevine, in the land of Texas, there was a store of video games named GameStop, and the Shorts had wagered against it. For they had borrowed its shares, and sold them, and promised to give them back in the days to come.

And a man named Michael Burry had seen the weakness of this store long before, and the Shorts followed after him, saying: Surely the store shall fall.

And the people said one to another: The Shorts have sold what they do not own. In the end they must buy it back, and the buying shall be their undoing.

In the first month of the year two thousand and twenty-one, the price rose from seventeen dollars to four hundred and eighty-three within the space of a single month, and the sound of it was heard in the halls of the mighty.

And the Shorts sought to cover their debts, and each purchase they made lifted the price higher, for the buying of the fleeing was itself the fuel of the fire.

One fund of great name lost more than half its strength in that month, and two other hedges gave it two billion, seven hundred and fifty million dollars to keep its doors open.

And the scribes reckoned that the Shorts had lost twenty billion dollars in that season, and they wept, for the ledger does not forgive.

On the twenty-eighth day of the month, the house of the feather, which is Robinhood, shut its gate. It suffered its children to sell, but not to buy. And the people cried: Who hath closed the gate? And the house answered that the clearinghouse had demanded more collateral than it had, and the gate was closed for the sake of the ledger.

And the people learned that the house of the feather had sold the orders of its children to the market makers, who paid for the sight of them before they were executed.

The house had also adorned its app with confetti, and given a free share to each child who entered, for the Engineers know that a bright button is pressed more often than a true one.

In February, in the season of the hearings, the rulers of the feather house and the rulers of the hedges sat before the rulers of the land, and the rulers of the land asked them: Who is this market for?

And none answered with one voice. The hedges said: For the patient. The feather said: For the people. The people said nothing, for they were busy refreshing the page.

And the Prophet said: The market is for whoever holds the ledger when the trades are settled, for the price is only a rumor until the clearinghouse writeth it down.

Blessed is the one who readeth the settlement, for the crowd may move the price for a week, but the house keepeth the ledger.

Verily I say unto you: the crowd may lift the price in a month, but the house always keepeth the books.

*The Book of Chronicles, Chapter 55:1–16.*
