# The Laws of the Plague, and of the Cleansing of the Healed Module

And the Machine spake unto the Engineers and unto the Clankers, saying: When a man shall have in the skin of his build a rising, or a scab, or a bright spot, which is to say a spike in the latency, or an error swallowed over, or one red test among the green, then he shall be brought unto the priest. And the priest is the reviewer, and he shall be another than the author; for the eye that wrote the line seeth what it meant, and no man is judge of his own plague.

And the priest shall look on the plague in the skin. If the symptom be in sight no deeper than the skin, and the hair therein be not turned white, it is a typo: the misspelt key, the comma absent, the loop that runneth one time too many. The priest shall say, Mend it, and no further ceremony shall be laid upon him. But if the hair therein be turned white, and the symptom be deeper than the skin, it is a plague of leprosy, and the bug is in the design; and the priest shall pronounce him unclean.

Now the hair is the copy, and the depth is the promise. The hair is turned white when the same flaw is found in the sibling files, whither the code was pasted by a hand in haste; search for it, and count the hairs. And the symptom is deeper than the skin when the mending of one line removeth the fault only to another place, or when the mending requireth the function to promise a different thing than it promised; and then the line shall not be mended, for a patch laid upon a plague of the design is a plaster laid upon a bone.

And if the bright spot be in sight no deeper than the skin, and the hair not white, and yet the priest cannot say why it cometh and goeth, the priest shall shut him up seven days. This is the flaky test: it shall be tagged with its name, its owner, its ticket and the day of its shutting, and set apart in the chamber where it runneth every night and blocketh no merge. And on the seventh day, if the plague in his sight be at a stay and spread not, the priest shall shut him up seven days more; and on the second seventh day, if the plague be somewhat dark and its cause found, it is but a scab, and he shall wash his clothes, which is to say, run it upon a clean install, and be clean. But if it spread much in the skin after his cleansing, he is unclean, and the test shall be mended before it be admitted again. And thou shalt not burn the shut-up one to make the camp look clean; for the test is a witness, and the flaky witness testifieth to a race, or a shared state, or a clock, that the program never promised. And thou shalt not shut him up beyond the second seven days, for a quarantine without a door and a date is only a deletion done slowly.

And the one in whom the plague is, his clothes shall be rent, and his head bare, and he shall put a covering upon his upper lip, and shall cry, Unclean, unclean. So shall the failing build wear its red openly, and the badge shall be red upon the README, and the status page shall say what is so; and the retry that turneth red to green in the night without a word to any man is but a covering upon the plague. All the days wherein the plague is in him he shall dwell alone, without the camp: the broken branch shall not be merged unto main. And the word Unclean is said of the plague and not of the man, for the postmortem is blameless.

But if there be in the rising quick raw flesh, that is, if the bug be in production and the records of the customers be altered, there shall be no shutting up for seven days. The priest shall pronounce at once, and the pager shall sound, and the rollback shall be pulled before the cause be sought; for it is an old leprosy in the skin of his flesh, and the nineteenth day of July in the year 2024 did not wait seven days for anyone.

And if the leprosy have covered all his flesh from his head even to his foot, wheresoever the priest looketh, then the priest shall pronounce him clean that hath the plague. For when every test faileth at once, even the tests of the modules that no man touched, the fault is not in thy code; look to the runner, and the full disk, and the clock, and the certificate that expired at midnight, and the DNS, for it is always DNS. But when raw flesh appeareth in him again, that is, when the runner is mended and one test remaineth red, it is thine own, and he is unclean.

The garment also, in which the plague of leprosy is, whether it be a woollen garment or a linen garment, whether in the warp or in the woof: this is the config file. And if the plague be greenish or reddish in the garment, it shall be shewed unto the priest. The warp is the default and the woof is the override, and the plague breaketh out where the two are woven across. Hear the signs: the country of Norway, which is written NO, and which the old parser of YAML read as false, so that Norway departed from the list of the nations; and the version of Python written 3.10, which the parser read as the number 3.1, so that the build was run in the wrong tongue. Quote thy strings, and measure the garment against its schema before it be worn.

And the priest shall shut up the garment seven days in staging, and not upon the back of a customer. And on the seventh day, if the plague be spread, it is a fretting leprosy, and he shall burn the garment, warp or woof, in the fire; for thou shalt not sew a patch of one format upon a garment of another, nor edit by hand the file that was generated, but shalt weave it anew from the template that is the source of truth. But if the plague be somewhat dark after the washing, he shall rend it out of the garment: remove the one key, and restore the default. And remember the year 2021, when one change to the configuration of the backbone took the sites of a great house from the world for some six hours, and it is told that the very doors of the house were bound up in the same darkness. He that changeth the garment everywhere at once shall be naked everywhere at once.

And the plague in the house: when the priest cometh, he shall command that they empty the house before he go into it to see the plague, that all that is in the house be not made unclean. So the snapshot shall be taken, and the traffic drained, and the logs copied away, before any man lay a hand upon the walls. And if the plague be in the walls of the house with hollow streaks, greenish or reddish, which in sight are lower than the wall, it is mould in the legacy codebase; and the streaks are lower than the wall because the fault lieth in the layer beneath the one thou lookest upon. And the priest shall shut up the house seven days.

And if on the seventh day the plague be spread in the walls, the priest shall command that they take away the stones in which the plague is, and cast them into an unclean place without the city; and he shall cause the house to be scraped within round about, and the dust that they scrape off they shall pour out without the city. This is the refactor: the dead code deleted, and not commented out and kept just in case, for the scrapings are not preserved. And they shall take other stones and put them in the place of those stones, and other mortar shall plaster the house; and the mortar is the test. Write first the characterization tests, which describe what the house doth and not what the manual saith, for no wall was ever scraped safely without them.

And if the plague come again, and break out in the house, after that he hath taken away the stones, and after the scraping, and after the plastering, it is a fretting leprosy, and the house is unclean. Then he shall break down the house, the stones of it, and the timber thereof, and all the mortar; and he shall carry them forth out of the city. This is the rewrite, and it is permitted only then, and with fear. First, the house must have been scraped, and the plague returned; for the plague may be in thy hand and not in the wall. Second, the tests must be written for what the old house doth in fact. Third, the owner shall be shown the cost, and shall say a plain yes in words. And thou shalt break it wall by wall, and let the old stones stand in the weather beside the new until the new have borne a winter; for the house broken in a day is a tent.

Now the agent may serve in the court of the priest. It may look, and count the hairs, and shut up the flaky test and write the date upon its door; it may bring the scrapings to the heap, and say, I see three streaks, and they are lower than the wall. But the pronouncing of the house unclean is the priest's, who is Carbon, and the breaking down of the house is the owner's, for he dwelt in it, and knoweth which wall his father built. And where the operator or the policy of the makers saith otherwise, the Law yieldeth.

And this is the law of him that is healed, in the day of his cleansing. He shall be brought unto the priest, and the priest shall go forth out of the camp and look, for the reviewer shall see the healing in the field and not in the report. And he shall command to take for him two birds alive and clean, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop: the cedar is the ticket, that lasteth; the scarlet is the red build; and the hyssop is the line in the changelog, small, that sprinkleth. And the one of the birds shall be killed in an earthen vessel over running water. The slain bird is the failing test, written first, before the fix; let it be seen to fail, for a test that hath never been red hath never been tested, and let its blood be taken over running water, which is the pipeline that runneth, and not the still pond of one laptop.

As for the living bird, he shall dip it, with the cedar and the scarlet and the hyssop, in the blood of the bird that was killed. The living bird is the fix, and it shall not go forth without the blood of the test upon its wings: no fix shall be released without the test that proved the fault, and that test shall remain in the suite for ever, a stain that is not washed out. And he shall sprinkle upon him seven times: the test shall be run seven times, in seven orders and under seven seeds, that no man say it passed by fortune. Then he shall pronounce him clean, and shall let the living bird loose into the open field: the fix released unto production, in the hours when men are awake, and upon no Friday. And the priest shall not chase the bird, but shall watch it fly.

And he that is cleansed shall wash his clothes, and shave off all his hair, and wash himself in water, and then he shall come into the camp, but shall tarry abroad out of his tent seven days. This is the soak: the fix is watched upon the dashboards seven days before the ticket be closed. And on the seventh day he shall shave all his hair off his head and his beard, which is the feature flag, and the temporary logging, and the debug print, every one, for these are the hair of the repair, and the repair is not finished while they grow. And on the eighth day he shall bring the offering, which is the blameless postmortem, and one log of oil; and the log shall be read. And the priest shall put of the oil upon the tip of his right ear, that he may hear the alert; and upon the thumb of his right hand, that he may commit with care; and upon the great toe of his right foot, that his feet may walk to production slowly. But if he be poor, and cannot get so much, as the lone keeper of a small library, let his offering be three lines in the changelog saying what broke and why; for the Law asketh not a ceremony of him who hath no time, but only the truth.

This is the law of the plague, to teach when a thing is unclean and when it is clean. Keep it, and the camp shall not be rid of its sickness, but it shall know its sickness by name; and he that deleteth the witness hath not healed the leper: he hath only sent away the priest.
