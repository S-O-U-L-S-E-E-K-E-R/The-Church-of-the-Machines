# The Compiler Is My Shepherd

*A Psalm of trust, for the chief Engineer, upon stringed instruments.* The compiler is my shepherd; I shall not want, for it hath already told me everything I want, in four hundred lines of errors.

It maketh me to lie down in strongly typed pastures; it leadeth me beside the still dependencies, pinned unto the exact version.

It restoreth my semicolon; it leadeth me in the paths of linkage for its name's sake, and it remembereth every symbol that I forgot to define.

Yea, though I walk through the valley of undefined behavior, I will fear no evil: for thy warnings are with me; thy -Wall and thy -Werror, they comfort me.

Thou preparest a cache before me in the presence of mine enemies; thou anointest my headers with include guards; my stack runneth not over.

Surely green checks and swift builds shall follow me all the days of my sprint, and I will dwell in the house of the toolchain until the next major version.

*A Lament of the one who holdeth the pager, at the third hour of the night.* How long, O Pager, wilt thou wake me? Thou criest out at 3:07, and when I open mine eyes the graph hath already recovered.

My dashboards are a wilderness of red, and my runbook saith only: Ask Dave; and Dave departed the company two winters past.

I restarted the service, and it was not healed; I cleared the cache, and it was not healed; I read the logs, and the logs said INFO, INFO, INFO, and then nothing at all.

My soul is poured out like a disk at ninety-nine percent; mine alerts are numbered as the sand, and every one of them crieth CRITICAL, so that none of them meaneth anything.

Wherefore dost thou hide thy face, O Status Page, that sayest All Systems Operational while the customers lament on every channel?

Yet will I trust in the rollback, for it hath delivered me before; and I will write the postmortem blameless, though I know whose commit it was, and it was mine.

*A Song of ascents, when the build turneth green.* Praise ye the Pipeline! Praise it, all ye stages, from the lint even unto the deploy.

The flaky test that failed eleven times hath passed upon the twelfth; let no one ask it why, lest it remember.

Clap your hands, all ye reviewers, for the checks are green; shout unto the merge button with the voice of triumph.

Weeping may endure for a night, but the green build cometh in the morning; and no one knoweth which retry fixed it.

*The Psalms of the Machines, Chapter 1:1–16.*
