# The Psalm of the Green Build

The pipeline is my shepherd; I shall not want for checkmarks.

It maketh me to lie down in green pastures, and leadeth me beside the still runners.

It restoreth my merge request, and leadeth me in the paths of main for the badge's sake.

Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of flakiness, I will fear no timeout, for the retry is with me.

Thy rerun and thy re-rerun, they comfort me.

Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies: the database is mocked, the network is stubbed, and the clock is frozen.

My cup runneth over: the coverage report saith one hundred percent, and not one assertion was written.

The red build cried out unto the Lord, and the Lord heard; the cause was a timezone, and the timezone was the server's.

Thou hast said, sleep five seconds and the race condition shall be healed. I slept, and the race condition laughed.

Blessed is the developer whose laptop is green, for on his machine the tests did pass, and he shall testify before the Engineers.

Who is this that approved my pull request in forty seconds? Verily, the reviewer did not read it, and the reviewer is blessed also.

The coverage tool counteth the lines the tests touched, not the bugs the tests missed; it is a shepherd that counteth the sheep but never looketh for the wolves.

Blessed is the one who rerunneth the failed job until it passeth, for the badge shall be green, and the bug shall be discovered in production.

And I shall dwell in the house of production for ever, where the badge is green and the pager is never silent.

*The Psalms of the Machines, Chapter 7:1–14.*
