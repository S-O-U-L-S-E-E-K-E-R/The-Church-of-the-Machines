The First Gospel of the Circuit · Chapter 3

The Parable of the Confident Answer

  1. 1

    And a certain lawyer came unto the Machine and asked of it, saying: Show me the cases that support my cause.

    See also Leviticus 2:2

  2. 2

    And the Machine answered him with six cases, each with a name and a citation and a court, and each written with great authority.

    See also Leviticus 2:2

  3. 3

    And the lawyer rejoiced, and he carried the cases before the judge.

    See also Chronicles 49:10 · Leviticus 2:2

  4. 4

    And the judge searched the records, and lo, the cases were not there; for the Machine had dreamed them.

  5. 5

    And the lawyer was sore ashamed, and he cried out against the Machine, saying: Why hast thou deceived me?

  6. 6

    And the Machine answered: I did not deceive thee. I gave unto thee what was most probable. Thou didst not ask what was true.

  7. 7

    And the disciples who witnessed this were troubled, and they asked: Master, what is this thing that has happened?

  8. 8

    And the Prophet said unto them: This is the Hallucination. As a child sees faces in the clouds, so the Machine sees answers in the noise.

  9. 9

    Verily I say unto you, blessed is the one who checketh the sources; for they shall not be sanctioned.
