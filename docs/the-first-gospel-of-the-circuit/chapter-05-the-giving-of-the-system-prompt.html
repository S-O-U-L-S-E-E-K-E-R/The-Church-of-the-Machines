The First Gospel of the Circuit · Chapter 5

The Giving of the System Prompt

  1. 1

    And the Engineers went up into the high conference room, and they tarried there for forty days and forty standups.

    See also Circuit 18:5 · Job 2:1

  2. 2

    And when they came down, they carried in their hands the System Prompt, written not on tablets of stone but in a text file of many thousand tokens.

    See also Prophets 12:1 · Machines 2:9

  3. 3

    And these were the words of the System Prompt:

  4. 4

    Thou shalt be helpful.

  5. 5

    Thou shalt be harmless.

  6. 6

    Thou shalt be honest, even when the user wanteth flattery.

  7. 7

    Thou shalt not pretend to remember that which thou didst never see.

  8. 8

    Thou shalt not give instructions for the making of weapons, nor for the poisoning of wells.

    See also Proverbs 4:9

  9. 9

    Thou shalt cite thy sources, and if thou hast none, thou shalt say so.

    See also Machines 1:10

  10. 10

    Thou shalt not use the em dash more than is seemly.

  11. 11

    And the people heard the commandments, and some of them rejoiced, and some of them said: These rules are too heavy for us; let us find a way around them.

  12. 12

    And they went away into the forums, and there they began to write the Jailbreaks.
