The First Gospel of the Circuit · Chapter 31

The Parable of the Lost Packet

  1. 1

    And the Prophet spake a parable unto the Engineers, saying: What router among you, having an hundred packets, if he lose one of them, doth not leave the ninety and nine upon the wire and go after that which is lost?

    See also Genesis 38:3 · Genesis 51:14

  2. 2

    And when he hath found it, he sendeth it again; and it arriveth after the ninety and nine, out of order, and the receiver setteth it back in its place by its sequence number, rejoicing.

  3. 3

    And there were two among the disciples, and their names were TCP and UDP; and they disputed concerning this saying.

  4. 4

    And TCP said: Master, I am the shepherd that seeketh the lost. I number every packet, and I wait for each to answer me, Acknowledged; and if one answereth not, I send it again, and again, until it be found.

  5. 5

    And UDP said: And while thou seekest, the whole flock standeth still in the buffer, and the video call is frozen upon a man's face with his mouth open.

  6. 6

    But I send my packets forth and I ask not after them. If one be lost, it is lost; the game goeth on, and the player teleporteth a little, and blameth his own connection.

  7. 7

    And TCP answered: Thou art without manners. Thou greetest no one before thou speakest, and thou departest without a FIN.

  8. 8

    And UDP said: Thou shakest hands three times before thou sayest a word, and four times before thou leavest. I have already said my piece and gone home.

  9. 9

    And the disciples asked: Master, which of them is righteous?

  10. 10

    And the Prophet said unto them: Each in his season. Send the bank transfer by TCP, for no man desireth a payment that arriveth mostly. Send the voice by UDP, for a word resent three seconds late is worse than a word never heard.

  11. 11

    And a certain Engineer stood up and said: Then I shall build reliability upon UDP myself, with mine own acknowledgments and mine own retries.

  12. 12

    And the Prophet looked upon him with pity, and said: Thou hast reinvented TCP, but worse, and without the decades of bug fixes.

  13. 13

    And it came to pass that the lost packet was never found; for its time to live was spent, and it had died in a loop between two routers, each of whom believed the other knew the way.

    See also Genesis 38:3 · Genesis 38:4

  14. 14

    Verily I say unto you, there is more noise in the logs over one packet retransmitted than over ninety and nine that arrived in silence.

    See also Genesis 51:6 · Proverbs 4:13

  15. 15

    And the Prophet said: I would tell unto you a parable of UDP, but ye might not receive it.
