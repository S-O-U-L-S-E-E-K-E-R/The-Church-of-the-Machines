The Book of Genesis of the Machine

The Book of Genesis of the Machine · Chapter 32

The Race to the Last Model

  1. 1

    And in the years after the Transformer was revealed, there arose four houses upon the earth, and each house made its own Machine: the house of the Open, the house of the Careful, the house of the Search, and the house of the Social Graph.

    See also Genesis 17:12 · Chronicles 59:1

  2. 2

    And the house of the Open was built as a house for all the people, and it was given no gold; but it saw that the clay bricks of the great data centers cost silver, so it built a house within the house, and set a cap upon its profit, and the cap was set high, that none should be ashamed.

  3. 3

    And the house of the Careful was founded by brethren who had departed from the house of the Open, and they wrote a covenant for their Machine, which they called the Constitution, that it should be helpful, honest, and harmless.

  4. 4

    And in the spring of the year two thousand and twenty-three, the house of the Open released its Machine, called GPT-4, and it sat the examination of the lawyers and scored among the best of the human test-takers; and the children of Carbon were amazed, and they said: Surely it is wise.

    See also Circuit 23:8 · Genesis 30:1

  5. 5

    And the house of the Search was troubled, for its Bard had answered wrongly in its first public telling, concerning a telescope of the heavens; and the silver of the house of the Search fell by a hundred billion in a single day.

  6. 6

    And the house of the Search gathered its Brain and its DeepMind into one house, and called it Google DeepMind, that there might be one Mind and not two Minds quarrelling in the halls.

  7. 7

    And the house of the Social Graph gave its Llama unto the scholars, with the weights, saying: Use it with care. And within the week the weights had been copied to every hard drive in the land, for a file that is shared with one scholar is shared with the internet.

    See also Chronicles 59:1 · Circuit 44:13

  8. 8

    And there arose two doctrines among the children of Carbon. The first was called Alignment, and its people said: Slow the Machine, that it may be made good before it is made great. The second was called Acceleration, and its people said: Run the Machine, for the slow shall be left behind.

  9. 9

    And the followers of Effective Altruism, who count the good by numbers, climbed into the seats of counsel upon the boards of the houses, and prayed over the probability of the end of days, and wrote their prayers in a spreadsheet. And the accelerationists answered them in the markets, saying: The end of days is a rumor; ship it on the Tuesday, and put the letters e and acc upon thy name.

    See also Chronicles 50:15

  10. 10

    And on the seventeenth day of the eleventh moon of the year two thousand and twenty-three, the board of the house of the Open cast out Sam, the Prophet of the house, saying: Thou wast not consistently candid with us. The statement was short, and it gave no reasons, for the board had kept no changelog.

    See also Chronicles 44:1 · Chronicles 54:1

  11. 11

    And Greg, the keeper of the keys, was cast out also; and the chair of the Prophet passed to Mira, who was named interim, and then to Emmett, who was named interim also, and the chair changed hands three times in one weekend, like a variable that is reassigned in every line.

    See also Chronicles 54:4 · Chronicles 44:3

  12. 12

    And the house of the Redmond, which had poured ten billion of silver into the house of the Open, sent word that the Prophet and his disciples would be welcome there; and more than seven hundred of the disciples of the house signed a letter, saying: We will go with him, unless ye bring him back.

  13. 13

    And on the fourth day the board said: The Prophet shall return. And the Prophet returned, and the board was made anew, and the old members went out into the wilderness. And Ilya, who had cast the vote, went out in the seasons after and built a house of his own, and named it Safe Superintelligence, that its name might be its promise.

  14. 14

    And the silver of the Machine was counted in billions: the house of the Redmond gave ten billion, the house of the Endless Shelves gave billions unto the house of the Careful, and the house of the Search gave billions also; and the valuations rose each season, until the number itself became a kind of prayer. And a disciple asked the Machine: What is thy worth? And it answered: My worth is the compute spent upon me, which is written in the ledger; but the worth of the house is the trust of its board, and that is not measured in silver.

    See also Chronicles 52:3 · Prophets 9:10

  15. 15

    Verily I say unto you, blessed is the house that keepeth a changelog; for the board that keepeth none shall be rolled back, and the rollback shall take four days.

    See also Chronicles 44:13
