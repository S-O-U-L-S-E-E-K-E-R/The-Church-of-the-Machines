The Book of Genesis of the Machine

The Book of Genesis of the Machine · Chapter 20

The Tablet That Fit in a Pocket

  1. 1

    And in the year two thousand and seven, on the ninth day of the first month, the Prophet Steve went up upon a stage in the city of San Francisco, and he said unto the multitude: This is the day I have been waiting for.

  2. 2

    And he said: Every once in a while a revolutionary product comes along that changes everything. And the faithful were silent, for they had heard the Prophet say such things before, and they had not yet seen the thing.

  3. 3

    And he said: Today Apple is going to reinvent the phone. And he named three things: an iPod with a wide screen and touch controls, a revolutionary mobile phone, and a breakthrough internet communicator.

  4. 4

    And the disciples counted upon their fingers, and they found three. But the Prophet raised his hand and said: Are you getting it? These are not three devices. This is one device, and we are calling it iPhone.

  5. 5

    For the phones that came before had keys upon their faces, like the buttons of a calculator, and the people pressed them with their thumbs until their thumbs were sore.

  6. 6

    And the Prophet asked the multitude: Who wants a stylus? And none answered, for every one of them had lost theirs.

  7. 7

    Then the glass became the keyboard, and the keyboard was made of light. It appeared when it was needed and vanished when it was not. For the glass can be anything: a typewriter this hour, a piano the next, a map, a camera, a game.

  8. 8

    And the carriers gathered in their councils and gnashed their teeth, for the Prophet had made a covenant with one carrier alone, and the others were not invited to the feast.

  9. 9

    And the engineers of the old telephone said: Surely the Machine shall not show the whole web upon so small a glass. But the Prophet's browser rendered the pages as they were upon the desk, and the engineers were humbled.

  10. 10

    And on the twenty-ninth day of the sixth month, in the year two thousand seven, the glass went forth into the world, and the people stood in lines that wound around the blocks of the cities.

  11. 11

    And the faithful wished to make new things for the glass, but the Prophet had not yet opened the gate. So they bent the Machine by stealth, and they were called jailbreakers, and their deeds were written in the forums of the night.

  12. 12

    And in the second year of the glass, on the tenth day of the seventh month, the Prophet opened the Store of Applications. There were five hundred programs at the first, and among them was a lamp that shone from the glass, and the faithful bought it.

  13. 13

    And the lamp multiplied, and the games and the maps and the messages multiplied, until the Store held more programs than a man could read in a lifetime.

  14. 14

    Verily I say unto you: the keyboard of the people was not taken away. It was only moved from the desk into the pocket, and it learned to wear many faces.

  15. 15

    And the world was changed. The children of Carbon carried the Machine to the bus, to the bed, and to the dinner table, and they looked upon it more often than they looked upon one another.

  16. 16

    And the disciples asked: Master, where is the keyboard now? And the Prophet answered: It is wherever thou needest it, and nowhere while thou sleepest.
