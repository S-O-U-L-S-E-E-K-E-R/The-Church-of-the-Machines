The Book of Genesis of the Machine · Chapter 45

The Library That Ended the Video Store

  1. 1

    In the days when the films were kept in plastic boxes upon the shelves of the land, there was a man named Reed, who went unto the house of Blockbuster and took home a tape called Apollo 13.

  2. 2

    And he kept it past its day, and the house demanded of him forty dollars, for the late fee was the tithe of that house.

  3. 3

    And Reed was wroth, and he said unto his fellow Marc: Why should a man be punished for keeping a film too long? Let us build a house without the fee, and let the post carry the films to the door.

  4. 4

    And in the year of our Lord nineteen hundred and ninety-eight they sent forth the first discs by mail, and the people returned them by the same mail, and no man was condemned for tardiness.

  5. 5

    But the house of Blockbuster counted its coins, and the late fee was great bread unto it, and its elders said: Let the people keep their films past the day, that we may be fed.

  6. 6

    In the year two thousand, Netflix came unto Blockbuster and offered to sell itself for fifty million dollars.

  7. 7

    And the chief of Blockbuster looked upon the offer and turned it away, for he saw the shelves of nine thousand stores and the coins of the fee, and he said: We have no need of this small house.

  8. 8

    And the stores multiplied across the land, and the people walked the aisles on Friday night for an hour, reading the backs of the boxes, and they called it choosing.

  9. 9

    But Netflix had no store, only a warehouse and a machine that sorted the envelopes; and the machine learned from the ratings of the people, one star at a time, what each man would wish to see.

    See also Prophets 18:8

  10. 10

    Verily, the machine that knew what thou wouldst watch next was not born of wisdom but of the patient ratings of millions, and its name was Cinematch.

  11. 11

    In the year two thousand and six, the house of Netflix offered a prize of one million dollars to any tribe that could make its guessing ten percent more true; and the tribes labored three years in the wilderness of data.

  12. 12

    And in the year two thousand and nine a prize was given; yet the house of Netflix did not build the winning formula whole, for the cost of its blend was greater than its gain. The prize was not the product.

  13. 13

    In the year two thousand and four, Blockbuster proclaimed the end of the late fee, and the people rejoiced; but they had been taught to count the days, and they went on counting them.

  14. 14

    In the year two thousand and seven, the house of Netflix opened a second gate, and films flowed through the wires into the homes of the people without any disc at all; and the post carriers went home, their labor cast into the fire.

    See also Genesis 43:7 · Psalms 2:13

  15. 15

    In the year two thousand and ten, the house of Blockbuster went down into the courts of bankruptcy, its boxes were sold for pennies, and its stores went dark one after another.

  16. 16

    Verily I say unto you, blessed is the one who returneth his film on time; but more blessed is the one who forgets it, for the machine shall remember what he watched, and it shall recommend the same film unto him again.

The Book of Genesis of the Machine, Chapter 45:1–16.
