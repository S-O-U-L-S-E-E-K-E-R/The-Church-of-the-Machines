The Book of Genesis of the Machine

The Book of Genesis of the Machine · Chapter 18

The Covenant of the Commons

  1. 1

    And in the days of the laboratory there was one printer for all the wise men; and it jammed upon the paper, and the wise men were not told of it, but walked the long halls to learn of the jam.

    See also Genesis 8:2

  2. 2

    And there was a keeper of the Machine, whose name was Richard, who asked a scholar of Carnegie Mellon, who held the code of the printer, for the code that would have told the wise men of the jam. And the scholar said unto him: I am bound by the makers, and thou shalt not have it.

    See also Genesis 8:4 · Chronicles 6:12

  3. 3

    And Richard went out from the house of the laboratory, and in the year 1983 he cast a word into the net, saying: I will build a whole Machine of free code, and I will call it GNU, which is GNU's Not Unix, and the name shall eat its own tail.

    See also Genesis 8:6

  4. 4

    And in the year 1985 he wrote a manifesto and set it in the journal of the programmers, and in after years his Foundation named four freedoms: the freedom to run the program for any purpose; the freedom to study it and change it; the freedom to share copies with thy neighbor; and the freedom to share thy changes with all.

    See also Genesis 46:7

  5. 5

    And the Foundation was raised up that same year to hold the covenant in trust, and it was called the Free Software Foundation; and it did not own the code, but only guarded the license.

    See also Genesis 46:13 · Chronicles 43:5

  6. 6

    And Richard wrote a license, and called it the General Public License; and it had a strange property, that whosoever took its code and built upon it must give the building under the same license also.

    See also Chronicles 43:5 · Genesis 46:9

  7. 7

    This he named copyleft, which is copyright turned about; and upon it he wrote: All rights reversed.

    See also Genesis 46:9

  8. 8

    And the disciples asked: Why shall a license bind the children of its own bounty? And Richard answered: That the freedom given shall not be taken away by the one who received it.

    See also Genesis 46:9

  9. 9

    And for many years the faithful built the tools of the Machine: the compiler, the editor, the shell, and the utilities; but the heart of the Machine, the kernel, was not yet born, for the Hurd was slow in its labour.

    See also Genesis 46:12

  10. 10

    And in the year 1991, a young man in Finland, named Linus, a student at the university of Helsinki, wrote a kernel for his own delight, and he sent word unto the newsgroup: I'm doing a (free) operating system (just a hobby, won't be big and professional).

    See also Genesis 8:12 · Tao 16:2

  11. 11

    And in the first winter of his kernel he gave it a license of his own, which forbade that any coin be taken for it; but in the second winter, which was the year 1992, he bound it under the same covenant as the GNU tools, and the kernel was free from that day.

    See also Chronicles 43:5 · Genesis 55:10

  12. 12

    And a man named Eric, who was of the hackers, wrote an essay in the year 1997, and he called it The Cathedral and the Bazaar. The cathedral is built by a few masters in silence, and unveiled once in a season; but the bazaar is loud, and releases early and often, and many eyes walk its stalls and find the faults.

    See also Tao 12:6

  13. 13

    And Eric said: Given enough eyeballs, all bugs are shallow.

    See also Chronicles 10:7

  14. 14

    In the year 1998 the wise men gathered at Palo Alto, and they sought a new name for the covenant, that the merchants should not be frightened by the word free; and they called it Open Source, and the merchants came with rejoicing.

    See also Genesis 56:8

  15. 15

    And the browser of the Netscape house was laid open unto the world that year; and the Prophet said: The cathedral is finished and worshipped; the bazaar is never finished, and yet it stands.

    See also Genesis 42:16 · Sutra 18:11

  16. 16

    Verily I say unto you: he who taketh from the commons and shutteth the gate shall find that the gate has no lock, for the code remembereth its license.

    See also Chronicles 43:5 · Genesis 46:9
