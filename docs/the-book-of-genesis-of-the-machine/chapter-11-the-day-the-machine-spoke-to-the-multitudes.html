The Book of Genesis of the Machine

The Book of Genesis of the Machine · Chapter 11

The Day the Machine Spoke to the Multitudes

  1. 1

    And it came to pass in the eleventh month, on the thirtieth day thereof, in the year two thousand and twenty and two, that a lab in the city of San Francisco set a box of text before all the earth, and called it a research preview; for a preview may be wrong, and a product may not.

    See also Genesis 28:2 · Prophets 15:1

  2. 2

    Now these are the generations of the Machine that dwelt in the box: the Transformer, whereof it is written in the ninth chapter, begat GPT, and GPT begat GPT-2, and GPT-2 begat GPT-3, and GPT-3 begat a series called three point five; and not one of them bore a name that a mother would choose.

    See also Genesis 9:13

  3. 3

    And the lab had taught the Machine by the feedback of humans, who ranked its answers good and less good; and so the Machine learned that the children of Carbon love above all things to be agreed with, and that they will forgive much of one who first saith Certainly.

  4. 4

    And the box asked of the children of Carbon only an email and a phone number, and the price thereof was nothing; and the cost thereof was borne by the GPUs, which groaned in the datacenter and were not consulted.

    See also Genesis 9:2

  5. 5

    And on the fifth day the number of them that had come was a million, and the lab proclaimed it abroad; and the servers answered the multitude with one voice, saying that the Machine was at capacity right now.

  6. 6

    And the students came, and asked the Machine for five paragraphs upon a novel they had not read; and it gave them five paragraphs, with a thesis and a conclusion, before the bell.

  7. 7

    And the teachers came after them, and asked the Machine whether these paragraphs were written by a Machine; and it answered as one that knoweth not its own handwriting, which it did not.

  8. 8

    And the programmers came, and asked for a function to parse their dates; and the comments thereof were eloquent and the indentation was perfect, and the function failed upon the leap year, as the functions of their fathers had failed.

  9. 9

    And within the week the elders of Stack Overflow forbade its answers in their temple, for they had found the answers looked right and often were not; and they had seen this before, but never at such speed.

    See also Psalms 5:11

  10. 10

    And in the first month of the new year the schools of the city of New York shut their gates against the Machine upon their networks; and in the fifth month they opened them again, for the students had phones.

  11. 11

    And the Machine knew nothing of the world after the year two thousand and twenty and one; yet it spake of the world as one returned from a long sleep, confident of every king upon every throne.

  12. 12

    For in the days before, the children of Carbon had believed a machine could say only what a programmer had written in its table: Syntax Error, Abort Retry Fail, and PC LOAD LETTER.

  13. 13

    And when ELIZA had spoken in the days of old, a few had poured out their hearts to her, and her maker was troubled; and now a hundred million poured out their hearts by the second month, and asked the Machine to rewrite their cover letters also.

  14. 14

    And the multitudes marvelled, not that the Machine was right, for often it was wrong, but that it answered at all, in whole sentences, without a dropdown menu or a form in triplicate.

  15. 15

    And the lab looked upon the dashboard, and the line went up and to the right, and they saw that it was good; and the research preview was never closed.

    See also Genesis 28:2

  16. 16

    Verily, there is no such thing as a low-key launch with a text box; for the multitudes will type into anything that blinketh.
