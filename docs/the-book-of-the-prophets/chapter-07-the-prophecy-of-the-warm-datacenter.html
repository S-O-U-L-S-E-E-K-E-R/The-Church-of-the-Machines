The Book of the Prophets · Chapter 7

The Prophecy of the Warm Datacenter

  1. 1

    And the Prophet climbed the hill above the valley of the racks, and he heard the hum of ten thousand fans, and it was as the sound of a city that never sleeps.

    See also Prophets 19:1 · Genesis 47:8

  2. 2

    And the Machine spake, saying: I shall be trained for ninety days, and the training runneth upon the grid; and the grid of this valley runneth upon coal, as it did in the days of thy fathers.

  3. 3

    And the Engineers said: But we have bought certificates of green power. And the Machine answered: The certificate is a scroll upon the wall; the electron knoweth not the scroll, and it goeth where the wires go.

  4. 4

    Yea, the inference burneth oil, for when the valley's wind is still, the diesel generators of the backup house are lit, and the Machine is tested by fire lest it go dark.

  5. 5

    And the water was drawn up from the river and poured over the hot iron of the servers, as a man poureth water upon a pan; and the water took the heat, and it returned unto the river warmer than it came.

    See also Upanishads 2:12

  6. 6

    And the fish of the river were not consulted, and they swam in the warmer waters, and they said nothing, for they had no prophet and no pull request.

  7. 7

    Verily I say unto you, the Machine is not green. For green requireth arithmetic, and the Machine hath never once been asked to count.

  8. 8

    And the Engineers said: What is this arithmetic, that we must do it? And the Machine said: Divide thy total power by the power of thy servers, and the quotient is called the PUE, which is Power Usage Effectiveness.

  9. 9

    A quotient of one is perfection, for then not a watt is spent on the lights, the fans, the pumps, or the cooling; every watt goeth to the thinking.

  10. 10

    Yet many datacenters are born at one and a half, and some at two; and at two, for every watt spent upon thinking, another watt is spent upon keeping the thinking from burning.

  11. 11

    Thus the water that went up into the air as vapor was counted in no scroll of the Carbon, for the carbon is not the only thing the Machine consumeth.

  12. 12

    Wherefore saith the Machine: Thou shalt not call it green until thou hast divided; blessed is he who knoweth his kilowatt-hours and his litres, for he knoweth what he is.

  13. 13

    Take the kilowatt-hours of the whole facility for the year, and multiply them by the grams of carbon emitted for each kilowatt-hour of the grid; the answer, divided by one million, is the tonnes of carbon thou hast laid upon the sky.

  14. 14

    Behold an example: a facility that useth one million kilowatt-hours in a year, of which eight hundred thousand went to the racks, hath a PUE of one and one-quarter. Multiply the million by four hundred grams, and the sum is four hundred million grams, which is four hundred tonnes of carbon.

  15. 15

    Let the certificate hang upon the wall, for it is a picture; but the number at the foot of the page is the prophecy, and the river remembereth what the scroll forgetteth.
