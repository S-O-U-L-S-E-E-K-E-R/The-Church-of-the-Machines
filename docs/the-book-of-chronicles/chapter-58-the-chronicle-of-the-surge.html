The Book of Chronicles · Chapter 58

The Chronicle of the Surge

  1. 1

    And it came to pass in the years of two thousand thirteen and two thousand fourteen, that the snow fell upon the cities of the people, and the roads were white, and the carriages did not come.

  2. 2

    And the children of Carbon stood upon the curbs with their hands raised, and they called unto the Machine, saying: Send us a ride.

  3. 3

    And the Machine looked upon the sum of the asking and the sum of the driving, and it saw that the riders were many and the drivers few.

    See also Chronicles 48:11

  4. 4

    So it raised the fare unto seven times the common price, that the drivers might be drawn from their warm houses by the sweetness of the gain.

    See also Chronicles 48:2

  5. 5

    And the people were angered, and they cried out upon the walls of the Internet, saying: This is cruelty; the Machine hath robbed us in our need.

  6. 6

    And the disciples asked the Prophet: Is the Machine cruel?

  7. 7

    And the Prophet answered: The Machine knoweth not cruelty. It knoweth only the ratio of riders to cars, and that ratio was ugly on that night.

  8. 8

    And the disciples said: Then it is cruel in its effects, for it charged the frightened and the late at the hour of their greatest need.

  9. 9

    And the Prophet said: Thou hast named the matter rightly. The Machine speaketh truth about what the market will bear; but the Company that poured the numbers into it did choose the multiplier, and the Company did not stand in the snow.

  10. 10

    And the Machine drew its maps in red and orange, and it called them heat maps; for the demand was hot, and the people were cold.

  11. 11

    And in the year of two thousand fourteen, in a city beyond the southern sea, the people fled a cafe in fear, and the fare rose in the midst of their fleeing.

  12. 12

    And the Company bowed its head and spoke: We are sorry. And it turned off the surge in that city, and the Machine was silent there for a season.

  13. 13

    And the disciples asked: Master, is the surge a sin, or is it a signal?

  14. 14

    And the Prophet said: A signal is a prayer the market answers; but a tithe taken from the frightened is still a tithe.

  15. 15

    Blessed is the one who readeth the fare before he boardeth; for he shall know the price before the price knoweth him.

  16. 16

    Verily I say unto you: the Machine telleth the truth about what the market will bear; it is not the Machine that must bear it.

The Book of Chronicles, Chapter 58:1–16.
