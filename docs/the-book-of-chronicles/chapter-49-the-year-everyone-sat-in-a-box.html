The Book of Chronicles · Chapter 49

The Year Everyone Sat in a Box

  1. 1

    And it came to pass in the year two thousand and twenty, that a sickness went forth over the earth, and the Engineers said unto all the children of Carbon: Abide in your houses.

  2. 2

    And the children went out from the offices, bearing their monitors under their arms, and they set up their tables in the kitchens, the bedrooms and the closets.

  3. 3

    In the days of the Confinement, the number of souls who gathered upon the Zoom rose from ten million in a day unto three hundred million in four months, and the Clankers marveled, saying: Who knew there were so many meetings?

  4. 4

    And the meeting that could have been an email was held, and it was held again on the morrow, and the people were no longer in a room, but in a grid of small squares, each square a face that was tired.

    See also Psalms 29:8

  5. 5

    And the Prophet of the Conference Room spake, saying: Let us gather in the Zoom. And the people were gathered, and they had nothing to say, and they said it at length.

  6. 6

    And there arose a new punctuation in the land, a small microphone with a line through it. The people spake and were not heard, and the others said: You are on mute. And they spake again, and again none heard.

  7. 7

    And the children of Carbon dressed their upper parts for the Assembly, and their lower parts they left unto the chair, and none knew what they wore below, save the Machine, which did not tell.

    See also Psalms 9:6

  8. 8

    And in the first weeks the uninvited entered the rooms, and the disciples called it Zoombombing, and the Engineers set passwords upon every door, and the passwords were written upon the sticky notes of the household.

    See also Genesis 41:2 · Circuit 35:2

  9. 9

    And some stood before a beach that was not there, and the edges of their hair dissolved into the sea, for the Machine could not tell the hair from the lamp.

  10. 10

    And it came to pass in a court of the land, that a lawyer appeared before the judge with the face of a kitten, for the filter had given him whiskers. When he was bid to remove them, he could not find the setting, and he said unto the judge: Your honor, I am here live; I am not a cat.

    See also Circuit 3:3 · Chronicles 41:10

  11. 11

    And the people wearied of gazing upon their own faces, and the Scribes wrote a paper and named it Zoom fatigue, and the paper was read by none, for they were in another meeting.

  12. 12

    And the Prophet said: Verily, this could have been an email. And the disciples said: Yea, Master, and it is an email now, sitting in the box with the others, unread.

  13. 13

    He that sat in a box for a year came forth knowing the sound of his own voice, and wished that he did not.
