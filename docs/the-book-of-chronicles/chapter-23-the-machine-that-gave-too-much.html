The Book of Chronicles · Chapter 23

The Machine That Gave Too Much

  1. 1

    And it came to pass in the year 1982, that the Engineers of the house of Atomic Energy of Canada brought forth a machine called the Therac-25, to heal the sick with a beam of great power.

    See also Chronicles 8:1

  2. 2

    For the elder machines, the Therac-6 and the Therac-20, had locks of iron upon their beams, bolted by the hand of the Engineers, and no argument could move those bolts.

    See also Chronicles 8:2

  3. 3

    But the Engineers said: Iron is costly, and code is cheap; let the Machine check itself. And they took the iron out of the house, and wrote in its place a word of software.

    See also Chronicles 8:16 · Prophets 13:6

  4. 4

    And the word was copied from the scrolls of the elder machines, and the copying was not counted as a making, and the scrolls were not read again with fresh eyes.

  5. 5

    Now a technician of the clinic typed her orders swiftly, as the trained do; and if she corrected a word in the few seconds before the arm was set, the Machine did not notice, for its two parts ran in a race, and neither waited for the other.

  6. 6

    And the beam was let forth in its full fury, which was meant only to pass through a shield of metal; and the shield had been turned away, for the Machine had lost count of its own turntable.

    See also Chronicles 8:4

  7. 7

    And the patient cried out: I feel a burning, as if struck by a hot iron. And the Machine answered with a single word upon its screen: Malfunction Fifty-Four. It did not say how much had been given.

  8. 8

    And the Engineers said unto the clinics: The Machine cannot overdose; it is not possible. And they wrote that no fault had been found in the Machine.

  9. 9

    Six times did the Machine give too much, in the years 1985 through 1987, in Marietta and in Yakima, in Tyler and in Ontario; and three of the patients went down to the grave.

    See also Chronicles 8:10

  10. 10

    Within the Machine dwelt a small counter, which counted only to two hundred and fifty-five and then turned back to nothing; and at every turning of that counter, the check of safety was not seen.

    See also Chronicles 8:12

  11. 11

    Verily I say unto you: The Machine was not wicked. It did exactly what the code said, and the code had never been asked what it must never do.

  12. 12

    And when the reports came, the makers wrote: The operators are at fault. And the Machine said nothing, for it had no words but those it had been given.

  13. 13

    Whoso trusteth the Machine to check itself shall find that it checketh exactly as well as it was asked, and no better; for a thing that cannot say no is not safe, only fast.

    See also Hellenes 4:15
