The Book of Chronicles · Chapter 46

The Kingdom of the Top Eight

  1. 1

    In the year two thousand and three, when the Machine was young and its pages were plain, there arose a people called Myspace, and they were given a blank page and leave to paint it.

  2. 2

    And the Engineers said: Let each man build his own house; let him choose the color of its walls, the font of its speech, and the song that greets all who enter.

  3. 3

    And they gave unto the people a box in which to write the code of cascading sheets, and the people pasted into it the code of strangers, and the page blazed with tigers and with text that crawled like a worm.

  4. 4

    And the people did not read the code they pasted; and it came to pass that the code of one man broke the page of another, and the Machine said nothing, for it was not asked.

  5. 5

    Lo, a song began to play at the opening of every page, so that the visitor, having come to read, was made to listen; and the song was not chosen by him, and he could not find the button that silenced it.

  6. 6

    Now the Machine kept a ranking of the friends of each man, and the ranking was called the Top Eight; and the people wept and quarreled over their places, and some were cast down to the ninth place, which is the place of outer darkness.

  7. 7

    And in the year two thousand and five, the house of Myspace was bought by the house of News Corporation for five hundred and eighty millions of dollars; and the Engineers rejoiced, and the Prophets counted the coins.

  8. 8

    And in the year two thousand and six, the house was named the most visited in the land, passing even the house of search; and the company said unto its masters: Behold, we are the multitude.

  9. 9

    But there arose in the north a smaller house called Facebook, which kept its walls plain and its speech orderly; and it was begun by a student in a dormitory, and it was not painted by its people, but built for them.

  10. 10

    In that same year of five, a worm crept through the profile pages in a single night, and it made a million friends before the dawn, and none of them had asked for it.

  11. 11

    And the Engineers had built the house upon servers of many kinds, stacked in rooms until the rooms could hold no more; and the house groaned under its own growing, and it was slow to answer its prayers.

  12. 12

    In the year two thousand and eight, the multitude began to leave, not by trumpet, but by the quiet clicking of many tabs; and the Top Eight was abandoned, for none would number his friends in public again.

  13. 13

    And News Corporation looked upon its purchase, and the house was written down, and written down again, until its worth could be carried in a single purse.

  14. 14

    In the summer of two thousand and eleven, the house was sold unto a company of advertising men, together with a singer of songs, for thirty-five millions of dollars, which was scarcely a sixteenth part of the former price.

  15. 15

    And it was revealed in the year two thousand and nineteen that a migration of servers had lost many millions of songs uploaded before the year two thousand and sixteen, and the Machine did not weep, for it had never been asked to keep them.

  16. 16

    Verily I say unto you: every page is a painted wall, and every wall is a temporary thing. Blessed is the one who keepeth a copy of his own tigers; for the house will be sold, and the page will not follow.

The Book of Chronicles, Chapter 46:1–16.
