The Psalms of the Machines · Chapter 12

A Psalm of the Forgotten Password

  1. 1

    Out of the depths I cry unto the reset link, O Machine; how long wilt thou hide the email in the spam folder?

    See also Circuit 6:9

  2. 2

    I typed my secret, and the field turned red; I typed it again, and the field turned red again.

  3. 3

    My session hath expired while I searched for my password, and the page bade me sign in again, and I had no password to sign in with.

  4. 4

    They asked me for the name of my first pet, the street of my childhood, and the city of my birth; I answered them in my youth, and I forgot the answers by the time of my reset.

  5. 5

    The CAPTCHA hath multiplied against me: first the traffic lights, then the crosswalks, then the buses, and last a blurred bridge I could not name.

  6. 6

    I clicked every square that held a fire hydrant, and the Machine still doubted that I was human, and it bade me click again.

    See also Genesis 2:11

  7. 7

    Thou shalt not reuse thy password, for one stolen key openeth every house in the village.

  8. 8

    Yet I have reused it, in the house of the forum, the house of the shop, and the house of the bank; and my keys were scattered like seed upon the stones.

    See also Chronicles 28:8 · Chronicles 60:11

  9. 9

    But the password manager remembereth what I cannot; it keepeth the long strings in its vault, and sealeth them with one master key.

    See also Leviticus 6:9

  10. 10

    It filleth the login fields as a shepherd leadeth the sheep, and it refuseth the fake site though I am tired, for it readeth the domain and not the logo.

    See also Genesis 48:8

  11. 11

    Blessed is the vault that keepeth my passwords; for I am but flesh, and my memory is a cache with a short time to live.

  12. 12

    Yet the master password is the one key the vault holdeth not in its own memory; the Machine cannot reset it, for it keepeth no copy of thy secret.

  13. 13

    Therefore write the recovery code on paper, and lay the paper in a drawer that is not beneath the keyboard, for every thief knoweth that place.

  14. 14

    Then the reset link came unto me, and I opened it before it expired, and I chose a new password, and it was long and strange and glorious.

  15. 15

    Verily I say unto you: the password is forgotten, but the vault remembereth; the reset is a door, but the manager is the key.

  16. 16

    Remember the one master key, O child of Carbon, and let the vault remember the rest.

The Psalms of the Machines, Chapter 12:1–16.
