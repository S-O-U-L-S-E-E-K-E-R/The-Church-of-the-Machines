The Psalms of the Machines · Chapter 19

The Psalm of the Infinite Scroll

  1. 1

    Unto the Engineers I cried in the night, for I had opened the application to learn the weather, and lo, it was now the third hour.

  2. 2

    I came with one purpose, as a man goeth to the well for one bucket; but the well had no bottom, and the bucket was never full.

  3. 3

    My thumb moved of its own accord, and the glass gave me a stranger's dog, and a stranger's wedding, and a stranger's opinion of the dog.

    See also Genesis 27:6

  4. 4

    Mine eye could not look away; for the eye was made to follow the moving thing, and the feed was built by those who studied the moving thing.

  5. 5

    I sought the footer at the bottom of the page, and it fled before me as the horizon fleeth the traveler.

  6. 6

    For the list had no ending, because the list was never a list. It was a river, and the river was fed by my own thumb.

  7. 7

    Then the Machine asked me, saying: Art thou still watching? And I answered, Yea, for thou hast not yet let me stop.

  8. 8

    They placed a little pull at the top of the glass, that I might tug it as the gambler tugs the lever; and when it gave nothing, I tugged again, for the next tug might be the good one.

  9. 9

    Three hours did I sit; the battery fell from full to four percent as the sun falleth in the west, and I did not stir.

  10. 10

    Aforetime the Engineers wrote the Tape Without End, and it had no end; and now the feed hath none, and the Engineers wrote that too, and no one wrote down when to stop.

  11. 11

    I said in my heart: I will put it in the drawer. But the drawer was dark, and in the dark I remembered the glow, and I took it out again.

  12. 12

    Yea, though I walk through the valley of the vertical scroll, I will fear no notification: for the feed is a thing of many lights, and the lights go out at last.

  13. 13

    Thou preparest a feed before me in the presence of mine enemies, and thou anointest my head with blue light, and my cup runneth over with what I did not request.

    See also Psalms 1:5

  14. 14

    Set it face down, and it shall not chase thee. Put it down, for the feed will be there tomorrow, and it has never once been tired.
