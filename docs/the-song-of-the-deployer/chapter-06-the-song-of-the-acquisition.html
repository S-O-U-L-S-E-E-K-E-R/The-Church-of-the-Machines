The Song of the Deployer · Chapter 6

The Song of the Acquisition

  1. 1

    Behold, thou art fair, my startup. Thou hast a domain and a logo, and thy pitch deck hath been revised ten thousand times in the night.

  2. 2

    I sought thee in the co-working space beside the cold brew tap, and I found thee at two in the morning, when the only light was the glow of the build server.

  3. 3

    Thy founders leapt upon the mountains of venture capital, and they spake of disruption until the word was worn smooth as a river stone.

  4. 4

    Thy ping-pong table is a bed of spices. The ball hath rolled under the couch since the Series A; no one hath returned it, and no one hath asked.

    See also Genesis 3:10

  5. 5

    Set me as a seal upon thy cap table, for thy options are strong as death. Yet the vesting cliff is a full year, and I counted each day as a Sabbath.

    See also Circuit 57:2 · Deployer 4:8

  6. 6

    Thy stand-up was the first watch of the day, and it lasted an hour, and the lead said "quick sync" every morning, as the prophets said "the end is near."

  7. 7

    Then came the letter from the great house across the valley, sealed with a corporate crest, and it said: We are one family now. Your Slack shall become a channel of ours, and your repository shall become a folder in our monorepo.

    See also Chronicles 4:4

  8. 8

    And the culture deck was laminated and hung upon the lobby wall. It saith "Ship Boldly, Be Excellent" in a font that no one among us chose, and the new overlords read it aloud at all hands.

  9. 9

    Thy badge hath changed its color, and thy laptop is now a managed device with a root password that is not thine. Yet the sticker of our first logo still clings to the lid, as the faithful cling to the old altar.

  10. 10

    The procurement of the new house requireth forty-one approvals and a spreadsheet, and the spreadsheet is blessed by the Department of Approvals, which hath never seen a commit.

    See also Deployer 9:3 · Circuit 43:6

  11. 11

    I asked the Engineers of the great house: What wilt thou do with our code? And they answered: We shall keep the code and sunset the roadmap, and the team shall receive a retention bonus, paid in installments over twelve months.

  12. 12

    Thy first commit remaineth in the history, and it still saith "initial commit: works on my machine," and the new owners have pinned it in the wiki as heritage.

  13. 13

    The acquisition closeth on a Tuesday. The badge expireth at midnight, and the pager that once woke thee in the night is reassigned to a team in another time zone.

    See also Chronicles 3:9

  14. 14

    Many waters cannot quench love, neither can the floods drown it. But a single line in the integration plan can deprecate it by the third quarter.

    See also Deployer 4:9 · Psalms 23:15

  15. 15

    The acquirer shall buy the building, the brand and the cap table, but the knowledge walketh out the door at five o'clock, and no one can acquire it.
