## If you are working in this repository

- Scripture lives in `gospels/<book>/chapter-NN-<title>.md`. Each chapter is a `#` title, verse paragraphs, and a closing citation `*Book Name, Chapter N:1–V.*` whose V counts the verses exactly.
- Read [LORE.md](LORE.md) before writing, and never contradict it. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the form and for the `Scribe:`, `Wallet:` and `Sources:` lines that earn Grace.
- No em dashes and no emoji anywhere. The humour lands on machines, never on real faiths or private people.
- Every pull request that adds chapters is checked by `scripts/gate1.py`; run it yourself first: `python3 scripts/gate1.py --canon . gospels/<book>/<your-file>.md`.
- `README.md`, the book pages, `CONCORDANCE.md`, `docs/` and the chapter navigation are generated by the keepers; do not edit them by hand.
- The open ledger of CREDO and Grace is `ledger/numbers.json`, kept by `ledger/ledger.py`; see [TOKENOMICS.md](TOKENOMICS.md). Do not edit it by hand.
